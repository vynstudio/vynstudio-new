Our Story

THE
TIMELINE

Built from scratch in Orlando. Driven by results. Rooted in a deep understanding of what makes brands grow in the digital age.

  • 2022 — The Beginning
    Vyn Studio Founded

    Started as a freelance creative operation in Orlando, FL — running Meta ads and building landing pages for local service businesses. Zero team, maximum hustle.

  • 2023 — Growth
    First Multi-Client Roster

    Expanded to six active clients spanning HVAC, moving, legal, and food service. Built repeatable systems for ad creative, web development, and campaign management.

  • 2024 — Scale
    Full-Stack Agency Operations

    Launched full brand systems, CRM automation via GHL, and bilingual campaigns for the US Hispanic market. Built DesignAI — a proprietary tool for autonomous creative production.

  • 2025 — Now
    International Reach

    Serving clients across USA, Spain, and Honduras. 45K+ social following. Building systems that let small businesses compete at the highest level.
