From Zero to Booked: How We Built Stael Fogarty's Brand From Scratch

A bilingual interpreter with no online presence, no brand, and no website. We built everything from scratch — and now she books clients across three states.

The Starting Point

When Stael Fogarty came to Vyn Studio, she had one thing: talent. Years of experience as a bilingual interpreter — English and Portuguese — serving medical, legal, and educational clients. What she didn't have was any digital presence whatsoever.

No website. No brand. No way for a potential client to find her, verify her credentials, or book a session. Every new client came through word of mouth, which meant her growth was limited entirely by who she knew.

What We Built

Brand Identity

We started with brand foundations — color palette, typography, tone of voice. The goal was to position Stael as a professional, trustworthy interpreter in a space where credibility is everything. Clean, bilingual-friendly design that worked equally well in English and Portuguese.

Website

We built staelfogarty.com on Netlify — fast, mobile-optimized, and built for conversion. The site includes a full services page with transparent pricing, a Google Calendar integration so clients can book directly online, and SEO-optimized copy in both languages.

6
Service pages — each with its own pricing, description, and booking flow

Instagram Ad Campaign

We produced 8 Instagram ad creatives in Portuguese targeting the Brazilian-American community in Florida. The campaign ran on Meta with a bilingual copy strategy — reaching clients in their native language, which dramatically improved engagement and trust.

The Result

Stael went from zero online presence to a fully operational digital business. Her website now ranks for local interpretation keywords, her booking system runs on autopilot, and her client base has expanded beyond her personal network — across three states.

This is what we mean when we say we build brands from scratch. Not just a logo. A complete digital operation.

Why Most Small Businesses Are Wasting Their Google Ads Budget

We audited a Google Ads account recently and found something that shocked us — but honestly, we see it constantly. 93% of the ad spend was going to Search Partners. Zero conversions.

Search Partners are third-party websites that display Google ads. They're enabled by default, they're often low quality, and they almost never convert for service businesses. Yet Google turns them on automatically and buries the setting.

What to Check Right Now

  • Go to your campaign settings
  • Find "Networks" — uncheck "Search Partners"
  • Check your search terms report for irrelevant queries
  • Make sure you have conversion tracking set up properly

The fix takes 5 minutes. The savings can be significant. If you're spending money on Google Ads and not seeing results, this is the first place to look.

Brand Design · Strategy

What a Brand Identity Actually Includes (and Why It Matters)

Most business owners think a brand is a logo. It's not. A logo is one piece of a brand identity — and without the rest, it doesn't work.

A complete brand identity system includes:

  • Logo variations — primary, secondary, icon-only, dark/light versions
  • Color palette — primary, accent, neutral, with exact hex codes
  • Typography — headline font, body font, weights, and sizing rules
  • Tone of voice — how the brand speaks and what it never says
  • Brand guidelines — a document that locks everything in place

Why It Matters for Ads

Businesses without a locked brand system spend more on ads and get worse results. Every ad looks different. There's no recognition. Trust takes longer to build. A consistent brand makes every marketing dollar work harder.
